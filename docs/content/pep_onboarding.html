Self Service Portal

The Self Service Portal is a great tool for managing software installation on your laptop. To access it, right click on the NOAA icon in your taskbar menu, and click on Self Service Portal. This will open a new window where you can add or remove OFIS-approved software without having to submit a Service Desk Ticket.

Service Desk Ticket

There are two ways to submit an OFIS Service Desk Ticket:

  • Email afsc dot servicedesk at noaa dot gov
  • Through the AFSC intranet

YubiKey

In addition to your user name and password, you also need a YubiKey to log into your Google account. The YubiKey needs to be brought with you into the field. If you have issues using it, contact OFIS with a Service Desk Ticket. If you are in the field and have issues with your YubiKey, ask OFIS to temporarily set you up with a different form of authentication for logging into Google (e.g., a code texted to your phone or recovery codes).

PEP Metadata

For our project and data metadata, we will update InPort as follows:

  1. S. Koslovsky and C. Christman will work with project leads to establish a metadata plan and to get started with InPort.
  2. The project lead will enter the appropriate metadata information into a GoogleForm that will be used to create and maintain program metadata records.
    1. The responsibility for creating, editing, and maintaining the GoogleForm entries is the responsibility of the project lead. Because these records will be available to the public and, in many cases, represent the authoritative documentation of the data set, project leads should devote an appropriate amount of time and thought to development of metadata records.
    2. Once the necessary and complete information is entered into the GoogleForm, notify C. Christman.
  3. C. Christman will complete the necessary updates/entries.

For more information on metadata workflow, review this documentation and/or contact C. Christman.
